Company Introduction / 公司介绍

HinYeung Technology was founded by a PhD team from The Chinese University of Hong Kong. We focus on the R&D and industrialization of high-speed, high-resolution 3D machine vision devices and intelligent robot systems, with the mission: "Empowering humans to use robots efficiently."
显扬科技由香港中文大学博士团队创立，专注研发并产业化高速高清三维机器视觉设备与智能机器人系统，使命是"让人类高效地用好机器人"。

Key Facts / 公司要点

- 3D camera accuracy: up to 0.5 μm (micron / sub-micron level) 精度达亚微米级
- 3D data acquisition frame rate: up to 310 FPS 采集帧率高达310帧/秒
- Applications: food, appliances, ceramics, logistics, education, new retail, automotive, 3C electronics, semiconductor 应用于食品、电器、陶瓷、物流、教育、新零售、汽车、3C电子、半导体等行业
- Offices: Hong Kong, Shenzhen, Foshan, Suzhou 香港、深圳、佛山、苏州均设有研发与应用中心
