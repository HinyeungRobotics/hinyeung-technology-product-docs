### 3. Force Feedback Robotic Arm
#### FrankaMate

Hinyeung’s FrankaMate teleoperation master controller is purpose‑built for Franka single‑ and dual‑arm robots. Industrial‑grade high‑precision joints, integrated force feedback and gravity compensation give it a generous working envelope, low control latency and responsive handling. It requires no manual holding or bracing, moves smoothly and delivers precise, immersive master‑slave teleoperation.

![FrankaMate Force Feedback Robotic Arm](./media/frankamate‑master‑arm.png)

### 4. Dual‑arm humanoid robot

Developed in‑house by Hinyeung Technology Limited, the dual‑arm humanoid robot combines high‑speed, high‑resolution 3D machine vision with 6‑axis robotic arms. It locates and identifies target objects on its own and completes delicate work such as grasping and assembly with motion as compliant as a human hand — well suited to education and research, smart manufacturing and human–robot collaboration.

![Dual‑arm humanoid robot](./media/dual‑arm‑humanoid‑robot.png)

### 5. Inspection robots (wheeled / quadruped)

Wheeled and quadruped inspection robots handle a wide range of terrains and can carry several models of robotic arm. They support autonomous patrol and perception, completing inspection and on‑site operations in a single pass.
